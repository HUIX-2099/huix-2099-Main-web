A HUIX-2099 game · Monrovia, Liberia

Experience the streets. Discover the hustle.

Monrovia Hustle is a game about the city of Monrovia, Liberia—its streets, its rhythm, and the hustle that keeps it moving. Step into a world made to be explored, where the place itself is part of the story.

The loop

Four words. One day in the city.

The poster says it in paint. In play, each word is a stage of the same run — and the run is what the game is built around. Keep scrolling: the city changes colour with it.

01

Step onto the street

Every run starts the same way: outside, in Monrovia, with whatever the day is already doing around you. Exploration is the heart of the experience — the city opens up as you learn it.

Open streets

World design · in development

The city as the starting point

Monrovia is not a backdrop here. The setting shapes the visual direction, the activities and what is possible during a run.

Where you go decides what you can do next — which is why the first hour of play is mostly about learning the streets.

02

Work the day

Routes, jobs and small trades. The city keeps score of what you do with it, and so does everyone else.

Your own plan
Every day brings a choice about where to go and what to take on. Player choice sits at the centre.
Trades and routes
Work that exists because the city does: moving things, holding a spot, knowing who to ask.
What people remember
The city keeps score of what you do with it, and so does everyone else.
  • Side jobs
  • Reputation

Systems · concept stage

The day as a set of choices

The hustle is the loop: a day with only so much time in it and several ways to spend that time.

Nothing is fixed to one route. The same day can be worked very differently by two players.

03

Make it count

Cash, respect and a name for yourself — the things you can actually carry home at the end of a run.

Cash
Pays for the next move: better routes, new ground, room to plan ahead.
Respect
How the city responds to you, and who is willing to help when you need it.
A name
The part that lasts between runs — what people say when you are not there.
  • Cash and clout
  • Buy your next move

Progression · being tested

What a run leaves behind

Earning is where the feedback lives: what you did today changes what is available tomorrow.

Progression is still being tested. What is described here is the intent, not a finished feature list.

04

Run it again

Tomorrow the loop starts over: different routes, different people, different consequences for the same choices.

Nothing wiped clean
The point of a run is to carry something forward into the next one.
New pressure
Routes open up, different people show up, and old choices come back with company.
Room to grow
A flexible identity that can carry different game formats without losing Monrovia.
  • New routes
  • New consequences

The loop · spine of the design

Why the loop is the design

The run — not a level — is the unit of the game. Four words describe one day, and the day is meant to be repeated.

It is also how the project is being built: a small, testable loop that can grow with each release.

The game

A game shaped by the city.

Monrovia Hustle is built around a recognizable urban world. The places you pass, the routes you take, and the life happening around you are all part of the experience.

The game is in development at HUIX-2099 in Monrovia. We are building it one step at a time, and we will only announce what is real.
